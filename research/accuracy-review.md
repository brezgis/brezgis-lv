# Historical accuracy review — September 2026

This model contains six eras. The central farm is an interpretive viewpoint near
Taurene, not a documented ancestral house at Brezģis. Existing research provides
regional analogies; it does not establish every object or date-specific footprint.

| Feature | Evidence and treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Timeline | Store calendar years −10800 and 50. The former values −12750 and −1900 mixed years before 1950 with calendar dates and produced incorrect transition labels. |
| Younger Dryas | Dwarf-shrub tundra and reindeer are environmental interpretation. Modern DEM, drainage and lake boundaries cannot prove their exact prehistoric form. Avoid asserting that nobody had visited the site. |
| AD 50 | Aurochs and a seasonal camp are plausible regional analogies; a place-name does not date an individual herd or settlement. |
| AD 950 | Āraiši is the architectural analogy. A sampled platform timber dates to AD 835; this does not date the simulated Brezgi farm. Defences, cemetery and neighbouring farm positions are interpretive. |
| Manor | The local authority records roof-raising in August 1888 and work continuing before 1900. Do not label 1888 as a precise completion date. The later brick manor remains absent in 1860. |
| Parish name | The council adopted Taurene with effect from 1 January 1926, following the 1925 proposal and recalling the medieval Tauren estate recorded in fifteenth-century documents. |
| Civic buildings in 1935 | The parish history identifies the old palace as a clinic and the old parish house at Tožas. It does not establish the new palace as the 1935 parish hall. Remove that claim and the interpretive civic flag from the new palace in this era. |
| Modern tower | Cēsis tourism records an 11 m observation tower opened in 2017. It belongs in the 2025 snapshot only. |
| Population | Taurene village and Taurenes parish are different units. The previous 347-versus-1,303 comparison did not establish a time trend and has been removed. |
| Modern vegetation | The 2020 Sentinel-2 mosaic guides the coarse woodland mask. Interpolation improves rendering, not the accuracy of the source classification. Individual trees are procedural. |
| Modern source dates | The 2025 scene mixes 2020 imagery and July 2026 OSM extracts. It is not a captured 2025 state; the panel and chronicle now state this. |
| P30 and bus stops | Preserve mapped surface/class tags and six bus-stop node IDs. Shelter/bench presence is sourced; shelter architecture and exact pavement details are inferred. |
| Water depths | River/lake geometry has a coherent rendered bed and depth-dependent shading, but depths are inferred from sculpted terrain, not measured bathymetry. |
| AD 50 camp equipment | Supports and roof slope rebuilt; no hanging metal cauldron is assumed at this undocumented camp. The shelter itself remains an illustrative regional analogy. |

Primary and institutional references checked for this review:

- [Taurenes pagasts — local authority parish history](https://www.vecpiebalga.lv/lv/vecpiebalga/vecpiebalgas-apvieniba/pagastu-vesture/taurenes-pagasts/): name, manor chronology and civic use.
- [Brežģa Hill — Cēsis tourism](https://visit.cesis.lv/en/place/brezga-hill/): tower height and opening year.
- [Meadows et al., Single-Year ¹⁴C Dating of the Lake-Fortress at Āraiši, Latvia](https://doi.org/10.1017/RDC.2023.24): platform timber AD 835; online 2023, journal issue 2024.
- [Akmens laikmets Latvijas teritorijā — Latvian National Encyclopaedia](https://enciklopedija.lv/skirklis/4433): earliest inhabitants and Stone Age chronology.

Remaining evidential limits: modern road alignments approximate historic routes;
the 1930s sheet constrains landscape structure but not exact property boundaries;
the DEM is sculpted for walkable channels and building pads; archaeology does not
establish the complete appearance of this terrace at any ancient date. Animal
positions, daily weather, lighting and sound are evocative simulation.

additional source links, visual changes and current limits of the procedural assets.
